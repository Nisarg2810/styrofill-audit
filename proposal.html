Questions

Good questions to ask.

Can't we just run Google Ads and skip SEO?

You can, and Ads will bring enquiries fast. But every lead costs money for as long as you run them. SEO and AEO build traffic you don't pay for per click, and it keeps growing. This programme runs both, so Ads carry you while SEO compounds.

Do we need a new website?

The current site gets fixed in the first two weeks so leads start coming in. A new fast, mobile-first website is then built in months 1–2 as part of the programme.

What do you need from us?

Admin access to the website, project photos and details for case studies (site, volume, density, client permission), and 30 minutes a month for a review call. We handle the rest.

What is AEO and GEO, in plain words?

AEO means getting your answers into Google's featured snippets and "People also ask" boxes. GEO means getting Styrofill named when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity for a geofoam supplier. Both come from clear, correct, well-structured content that other sites cite.

How much should we spend on Google Ads?

We'll recommend a starting budget after keyword research, then track the cost per enquiry from week one and adjust it based on real numbers. The spend goes straight to Google from your own account.
